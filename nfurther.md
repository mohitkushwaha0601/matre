The guide is structured around chronology, colonial administration, economics, social change, 1857, INC, revolutionary movements, Gandhi, Bose/INA, constitutional evolution, both World Wars, communal politics, Cabinet Mission, Partition, military dimensions, international factors, historiographical controversies, lesser-known dimensions, and SSB-specific analytical/debate questions.

I cross-checked the framework against NCERT's modern-history themes, British Library India Office records, the Government of India Act 1935, Nehru Archive primary documents, and Ministry of Culture material. For example, the British Library catalogue contains dedicated Indian Independence Bill/transfer-of-power records and contemporary 1857 material, while the Nehru Archive preserves primary documents showing the competing Congress, League and British positions during the Cabinet Mission period. ([British Library Archives][1])

### A useful common structure for all 10

For your SSB preparation, I'd actually recommend keeping **every geopolitical document in the same format**:

**History → Geography → Actors → Interests → Military balance → Economics → Alliances → Timeline → Current developments → Competing narratives → International law → India's interests → Risks → Strategic options → Lesser-known facts → SSB questions → Debate topics → 2-minute answer framework → Rapid revision sheet.**

That will make the entire set much easier to revise as a single **SSB Strategic Affairs library**, rather than ten disconnected PDFs.
