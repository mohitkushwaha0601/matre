Part I · Land and life

The ground and what lives on it

District, Ghats and geology

  • Chikkamagaluru district lies in south-western Karnataka. Name tradition: "younger daughter's town". Tradition
  • Two zones: Malnad (wet, forested hills, west) and Bayaluseeme (drier plains, east).
  • High points: Mullayanagiri (about 1,930 m, usually listed as the highest peak in Karnataka), Baba Budan Giri, and the Kudremukh range (main peak about 1,894 m; "horse face"). Verify
  • The Western Ghats (Sahyadri) run about 1,600 km along the west coast. They catch the south-west monsoon and form a steep escarpment on the coastal side. Verify
  • Kudremukh rock is old Precambrian (Dharwar craton) with banded iron formation (magnetite-quartzite), the basis of the iron-ore mining.

Ecology, wildlife and climate

  • Shola-grassland mosaic: patches of stunted evergreen forest in folds between open grass hills. The grass absorbs monsoon rain and releases it slowly to streams.
  • Wildlife: lion-tailed macaque, Malabar giant squirrel, gaur, sambar, leopard, tiger, many endemic amphibians and plants. The Ghats are a biodiversity hotspot. Verify
  • Monsoon (Jun-Sep): very heavy rain, mist, leeches, fast streams; trekking often restricted.
  • Oct-Feb: usual trekking window. Mar-May: hot, dry grass, fire risk. Verify

Part II · Water and people

Rivers, ore, protection and heritage

Netravati, Tunga and Bhadra

  • Netravati rises in the Kudremukh range, flows west through Dakshina Kannada, joins the Kumaradhara at Uppinangady and meets the Arabian Sea at Mangaluru. Verify
  • Tunga and Bhadra rise at Gangamoola in the same range, flow east and join near Koodli to form the Tungabhadra, which passes Hampi (handbook 44) and joins the Krishna. Verify
  • One range, two destinations: a drainage divide between the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.
  • Mangaluru has the New Mangalore Port and Coast Guard presence, linking these hills to coastal security (handbook 34).

Mining, national park and World Heritage

  • From the 1970s KIOCL mined magnetite at Kudremukh; slurry went by pipeline to the coast near Mangaluru for pelletising and export. Verify
  • Concern: forest loss, tailings and siltation in the Bhadra catchment. After litigation the Supreme Court directed mining to end; it stopped around 2005. Verify
  • Kudremukh National Park was notified in the late 1980s; Bhadra (Chikmagalur) is a tiger reserve. Verify
  • National parks are the strictest category under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972: entry by permission, no extraction.
  • The Western Ghats were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage serial site in 2012. Verify which Kudremukh parts are covered.

For mining

Jobs, exports, township, public-sector industry.

Against mining

Habitat loss, tailings, damage to a river source, irreversible loss.

Faith, history and coffee

  • Coffee: tradition says Baba Budan brought seeds from Yemen to the hills named after him around the 17th century. Tradition Karnataka is India's largest coffee producer; estates are shade-grown with pepper and cardamom.
  • Baba Budan Giri shrine is revered by Hindus (Dattatreya Peetha) and Muslims, with periodic disputes over access and rites. Disputed
  • Sringeri (Sharada Peetha) in the district has links with early Vijayanagara rulers (handbook 44). Horanadu has the Annapoorneshwari temple.
  • Estates, temples and forests share the same hills, so trekkers should respect local land and rules.

Part III · Trek and SSB link

On the trail

The trek: peak, season, permits

  • Netravati Peak is a trekking destination in the Kudremukh range: grass slopes, ridge views, mist, named after the nearby river. Expect forest sections, exposed grassland, steep climbs and no phone signal. Verify height, route length, trailhead and camping rules; sources use different names for peaks here.
  • Permit from the Karnataka Forest Department (often online) is normally needed, sometimes with a compulsory guide and group-size limit. Verify
  • Season: post-monsoon Oct-Feb is usual; check closure dates.
  • Kit: grippy shoes, rain jacket, warm layer, 2-3 litres of water, snacks, headlamp, whistle, first-aid, offline and paper map, leech socks in wet months.
  • Plan: start early, share route and return time, build fitness beforehand.

Safety and leave-no-trace

  • Hazards: slippery slopes, mist and getting lost, flash-rising streams, heat and dehydration, leeches, wildlife.
  • If lost: stay together, stop in poor visibility, signal with a whistle (three blasts), call the forest office or local emergency number. Verify
  • Know first aid for sprains, cuts, heat exhaustion and hypothermia.
Leave no trace

Carry out all waste, avoid plastic, stay on trail, do not feed animals or pick plants, avoid fires and noise. Protected-area rules can carry penalties.

Terrain, navigation and OLQs

  • Contours join points of equal height: close lines mean steep slope. Spur = ridge projecting from high ground; re-entrant = fold between spurs where water collects; saddle = low point between peaks.
  • Navigation: map, compass, bearing, handrail features, pace-count and time; in mist, trust bearing and pace.
  • Military link: ridges give observation, valleys carry routes and water, weather affects both.
  • OLQs shown: planning and organising (kit, route), cooperation (helping the slowest), determination and stamina (climbs), presence of mind and courage (mist, injury), responsibility (forest rules), effective intelligence (reading weather and ground).
  • Injury drill: assess, decide, act, communicate, review.

Analysis and practice

Perspectives and scenarios

Conservation first

Source forests protect rivers and species; losses are irreversible; keep tourism small and controlled.

Livelihood and access

Communities, estates and guides depend on the land and visitors; blanket bans can hurt them.

  • Disputed How many trekkers a fragile grassland can bear.
  • Interp Whether mining closure was a net gain after counting jobs lost and ecology saved.
  • Fact The Netravati, Tunga and Bhadra rise in the same range but reach different seas.

Overcrowding

Daily caps, online slots, guides, fees for trail repair, rest closures.

Dry-season fire

Seasonal closure, fire lines, local teams, no-fire rules.

Lost in mist

Stay put, signal, bearing back to last known point, call forest office.

Key terms

Malnad
Hill country of the Karnataka Ghats.
Shola
Stunted evergreen forest patch in a sheltered fold.
Escarpment
Long steep slope between high and low ground.
Drainage divide
Ridge sending water to different basins.
Tailings
Waste left after separating ore from rock.
Bearing
Direction as an angle from north.
