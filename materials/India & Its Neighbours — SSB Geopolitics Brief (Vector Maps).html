Key Concepts

Two-Front Threat

Pakistan (west) + China (north): both nuclear-armed, both cooperating (CPEC). India's theatre command reforms are a direct response to this dual threat.

Chicken's Neck (Siliguri)

~22 km wide corridor connecting Northeast India to mainland. Between Nepal and Bangladesh. India's most critical geographic vulnerability — must never be severed.

Buffer State Strategy

Nepal and Bhutan are India's strategic buffers. Doklam (2017) — India intervened in Bhutanese territory because it was too close to the Siliguri Corridor.

Disputed Territories

Pakistan claims all of J&K. China claims Arunachal Pradesh and holds Aksai Chin (since 1962). India's position: both are integral — non-negotiable.

SSB Angle

Know where Depsang, Shaksgam Valley, and Chumbi Valley are. These are the next potential flashpoints — named locations signal strategic awareness to evaluators.

Kashmir — Core Concepts

Why Kashmir is Unresolved

Maharaja Hari Singh's Instrument of Accession (Oct 1947) after Pakistani tribal invasion. Pakistan never accepted it. Nehru's UN referral — widely seen as a strategic error.

Article 370 Abrogation (2019)

J&K bifurcated into two UTs. Pakistan downgraded ties, suspended trade. China objected. Constitutionally contested but strategically decisive.

CPEC Through PoK

China-Pakistan Economic Corridor passes through Gilgit-Baltistan (India's claim). CPEC legitimises Pakistan's occupation and builds permanent Chinese infrastructure in disputed territory.

Nuclear Threshold

Pakistan's Nasr tactical missile was designed to deter India's Cold Start. Any large-scale Indian conventional response risks Pakistan crossing the nuclear threshold.

SSB Angle

"Would you want to be posted in J&K?" — Show genuine eagerness, terrain awareness, and strategic maturity. Never reluctance, never blind aggression.

China Border — Key Concepts

Why LAC is Never "Resolved"

Never formally surveyed or demarcated. Both nations have different maps. Every patrol is a potential confrontation. China exploits this ambiguity deliberately through grey-zone incursions.

Salami Slicing

Taking territory in tiny, individually insignificant slices so no single act justifies military response. Depsang, Galwan, Pangong — each was a slice. Cumulative effect: permanent status quo change.

Infrastructure Race

China built world-class roads, villages, and airstrips in Tibet over decades. India's BRO is racing to catch up: Atal Tunnel, DBO road, Z-Morh Tunnel, Sela Tunnel. Roads = response time.

Brahmaputra as a Weapon

China's Motuo Dam (60 GW) on Yarlung Tsangpo. Controlled water releases could flood Assam or deny irrigation — a strategic weapon requiring zero military mobilisation.

India's Counter

Agni-V ICBM (covers all of China), BrahMos on LAC, BRO fast-tracking, QUAD, trade decoupling, arming China's neighbours (Vietnam, Philippines).

Indian Ocean — Key Concepts

String of Pearls

China's port network (Gwadar → Colombo → Chittagong → Kyaukpyu) encircling India. Commercial now, potentially military later. The infrastructure is being built before the intent is declared.

Malacca Dilemma

80% of China's oil passes through Malacca — which India flanks with Andaman & Nicobar Command. China can't fight India and secure its oil simultaneously. Hence Gwadar (west bypass) and Kyaukpyu (east bypass).

SAGAR Doctrine

Security And Growth for All in the Region (2015). Make India the preferred security partner for Indian Ocean islands. Counter to BRI without naming it.

QUAD's Maritime Role

QUAD is fundamentally a maritime alliance: freedom of navigation, shared domain awareness (tracking Chinese ships), interoperability. US Pacific Fleet as de facto counterweight.

SSB GD Angle

"Militarise Andaman & Nicobar?" — India's most powerful card. But full militarisation complicates ASEAN relations. Calibrated upgrading keeps China guessing without alarming neighbours.

Policy & Doctrine

India's Strategic Doctrines

Cold Start Doctrine

India's limited-war doctrine vs. Pakistan. Swift, shallow penetration into Pakistani territory before international pressure forces a ceasefire — denying Pakistan the "nuclear threshold" justification. Operationalised through Integrated Battle Groups (IBGs).

Neighbourhood First Policy

Launched by PM Modi (2014). Prioritises SAARC neighbours in foreign policy. Aims to reduce China's influence by making India the preferred partner in South Asia through connectivity, economic aid, and cultural exchange.

SAGAR Doctrine

Security And Growth for All in the Region (2015). Keeps the Indian Ocean free, open, and prosperous. Counters String of Pearls by building security partnerships with island nations — Maldives, Mauritius, Seychelles, Sri Lanka, Madagascar.

Strategic Autonomy

India refuses to join any military alliance while maintaining deep partnerships with the US, Russia, France, and others. Participates in QUAD while keeping the Russian S-400. The art of keeping all options open simultaneously.

Act East Policy

Deepening engagement with ASEAN, SE Asia, and East Asia. Counters China's influence via Kaladan project, BIMSTEC, and Vietnam defence ties — India arming China's neighbours without formally announcing it.

Two-Front War Preparedness

India's military prepares for simultaneous conflict on the western (Pakistan) and northern (China) fronts. Galwan 2020 accelerated this: faster BRO roads, forward deployments, integrated theatre commands, Agni-V deployment.

SSB Officer's Corner

"Biggest security threat — Pakistan or China?"

Model Answer Cue
Both — differently. China is the long-term structural challenge; Pakistan is immediate friction. India prepares for both simultaneously. Show dual-front complexity.

"Was the Balakot airstrike the right decision?"

Model Answer Cue
It permanently changed India's deterrence posture — targeted, proportionate, not an act of war. The key was measured response. Avoid both uncritical praise and second-guessing.

"What is the Malacca Dilemma?"

Model Answer Cue
80% of China's oil passes through Malacca — flanked by India's Andaman & Nicobar Command. India's strategic leverage over China's energy security. Must remain a credible threat.

"Should India maintain No-First-Use nuclear policy?"

Model Answer Cue
NFU is India's stated doctrine. But Pakistan's Nasr tactical nukes challenge it. Show you understand the tension between NFU's diplomatic value and military limitations.

"How to respond to China's salami-slicing?"

Model Answer Cue
Counter-salami-slicing: match every patrol, build infrastructure faster, maintain forward presence. Diplomatically: raise costs through QUAD, trade decoupling, arming China's neighbours.

"Is QUAD an alliance against China?"

Model Answer Cue
India's official position: not an alliance, not against any country. But acknowledge the reality: China's assertiveness is why it was revived in 2017. Show the gap between diplomatic language and strategic intent.

Group Discussion Topics

QUAD vs Non-Alignment

Is QUAD a compromise of strategic autonomy? QUAD offers security; non-alignment offers flexibility and Russia ties.

Water Wars in South Asia

Brahmaputra (China), Teesta (Bangladesh), Indus (Pakistan). Water as a geopolitical weapon — India's strategic options.

Militarise Andaman & Nicobar?

India's most powerful maritime card. Strategic gains vs. diplomatic costs with ASEAN — where is the right balance?

SAARC vs BIMSTEC

SAARC paralysed by Pakistan. Should India fully pivot to BIMSTEC as primary regional architecture?

India-China: Trade & Rivalry

$120 billion bilateral trade alongside territorial conflict. Can two nuclear-armed rivals trade and compete without war?

Article 370: Gains & Costs

Did abrogation strengthen India's Kashmir position or create new strategic vulnerabilities (China-Pakistan alignment)?
