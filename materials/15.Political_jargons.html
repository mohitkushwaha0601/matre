Every political term answers one question

Confusion comes from treating all these words as one list. They sit on different dimensions: economy, who rules, how much power, values, and state structure. Pick a question below to filter, then read each term with its family of branches.

Real countries combine one answer from each dimension.
India: mixed economy + democratic republic + federal + secular.
UK: mixed economy + parliamentary democracy + constitutional monarchy + unitary state.
Saudi Arabia: market economy + absolute monarchy + religion-based state.
China: state-guided market economy + one-party authoritarian rule + officially communist.
