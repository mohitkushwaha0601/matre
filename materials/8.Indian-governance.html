Recent Inductions & Defence Activity

Indigenous capability, Aatmanirbhar Bharat, and what entered service in 2026
27 FEB 2026 · CHANDIPUR, ODISHA

VSHORADS flight trials completed

DRDO (led by Research Centre Imarat) completed three consecutive trials of the indigenous Very Short Range Air Defence System — a man-portable system to intercept fast, low aerial threats. Cleared for induction into the armed forces.

6 JUN 2026 · GOA

First indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle (H-561) inducted

Coast Guard hovercraft, first of a planned six-ACV fleet, built for shallow water, marshy and mudflat operations — coastal surveillance, search & rescue, disaster response.

6 JUN 2026 · HAZIRA / NEW DELHI

TEJASTRA laser system showcased

High-Energy Laser System for counter-drone and air-defence roles displayed during a PM Modi visit, alongside other next-gen indigenous arsenal.

12 JUN 2026 · HYDERABAD

Advanced Weapon System Complex inaugurated at DRDL

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh opened the facility at the Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Missile Complex, crediting Akash and BrahMos performance in Operation Sindoor as proof of India's defence R&D strength.

7 JUN 2026 · ARMY CHIEF INTERVIEW

Zorawar light tank timeline slips to 2028–29

The DRDO–L&T mountain-warfare tank (meant to speed up armoured deployment against China along the LAC) needs further design refinement; induction pushed from 2027 to 2028–29.

29 JUN 2026 · RASHTRAPATI BHAVAN

Defence Investiture Ceremony 2026 (Phase II)

President Murmu conferred gallantry and distinguished service awards, including a Shaurya Chakra for courageous conduct during Operation Chuntawadi in J&K.

Geopolitical Tensions & India's Perspective

The fault lines an interviewing officer expects you to be able to discuss calmly
PAKISTAN

Operation Sindoor (May 2025) and its aftermath

Following the Pahalgam terror attack (22 Apr 2025, 26 civilians killed), India struck nine terror-linked sites in Pakistan and PoK on 7 May 2025 — including Jaish-e-Mohammed's Bahawalpur base and Lashkar-e-Taiba's Muridke base. A short aerial exchange followed before a ceasefire. China's technical support to the Pakistan Air Force during the conflict was later confirmed, and the Indus Waters Treaty has been kept in abeyance citing Pakistan-backed terrorism.

India's perspective: a declared shift from "strategic restraint" to a calibrated, zero-tolerance military response doctrine on cross-border terrorism — paired with continued emphasis on indigenous strike capability (Akash, BrahMos) as proof of self-reliant deterrence.
CHINA

LAC — cautious thaw, periodic flare-ups

After the 2020 Galwan clash, an October 2024 patrolling agreement enabled disengagement at Depsang and Demchok. Reports of renewed friction near Taksing (Arunachal Pradesh) surfaced in July–August 2026, timed just ahead of India hosting the BRICS Summit in New Delhi (Sep 2026), where a Modi–Xi meeting was anticipated.

India's perspective: manage the border through structured dialogue (WMCC, Corps Commander talks) while continuing military modernisation for high-altitude terrain (Zorawar tank programme) — normalising ties without lowering guard, especially given China's parallel defence ties with Pakistan.
INDO-PACIFIC & STRATEGIC AUTONOMY

Balancing the Quad, Russia, and BRICS

India deepens Quad-linked exercises (Malabar, Austra Hind, Pitch Black) to hedge against Chinese naval expansion, while keeping the Russia channel alive (Indra) and signing new defence/cyber pacts (e.g., an India–South Korea MoU in May 2026). India hosted the 2026 BRICS Summit, underscoring its "multi-aligned" posture.

India's perspective: strategic autonomy — partner with whoever advances Indian interests on a given issue, rather than binding into one bloc.

Nuclear balance (context, not a talking point to over-emphasise)

SIPRI's 2026 Yearbook estimated India's stockpile at roughly 190 warheads against Pakistan's roughly 170 — part of a broader, decades-long modernisation rather than any single event.

Turning This Into SSB Marks

How to actually use this dossier in GD, lecturette and interview

Likely GD / Lecturette topics

  • Tri-service integration and the role of the CDS
  • India's shift from "strategic restraint" to calibrated response
  • Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence manufacturing
  • Balancing China engagement with border vigilance
  • India's "multi-alignment" — Quad, Russia, BRICS together

Interview — be ready to state cold

  • Current CDS, three Service Chiefs, Defence Secretary, Raksha Mantri
  • What CDS/DMA does that the three Chiefs individually don't
  • One indigenous system inducted this year and why it matters
  • One India-hosted or India-led exercise and its strategic purpose
  • The one-line difference between the LoC and the LAC

Pre-board checklist

  • Can name all three Service Chiefs, the CDS, and the Defence Secretary without hesitation
  • Can explain in two sentences why the Army Chief being from the Armoured Corps is notable
  • Can describe Operation Sindoor factually, without editorialising or inflaming rhetoric
  • Can name at least one Quad-linked exercise and one neighbourhood-diplomacy exercise
  • Has re-checked this dossier's names against a fresh search within a week of the board date
