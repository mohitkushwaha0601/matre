03

Commands & Force Structure

Indian Army — 7 Commands

Northern (Udhampur), Western (Chandimandir), Eastern (Kolkata), Southern (Pune), South-Western (Jaipur), Central (Lucknow), and Army Training Command (Shimla).

Indian Navy — 3 Commands

Western Naval Command (Mumbai), Eastern Naval Command (Visakhapatnam), and Southern Naval Command (Kochi).

Indian Air Force — 7 Commands

Western, Eastern, Central, Southern, South-Western, Training Command, and Maintenance Command.

Unified / tri-service

Andaman & Nicobar Command, Strategic Forces Command, and the Department of Military Affairs under the CDS.

Note: individual GOC-in-C and Flag Officer names rotate frequently; verify the current incumbents close to your board date.

Recent Inductions & Defence Activity

27 FEB 2026 · CHANDIPUR

VSHORADS flight trials completed

DRDO completed three consecutive trials of the indigenous Very Short Range Air Defence System, a man-portable system designed to intercept low, fast aerial threats.

6 JUN 2026 · GOA

First indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle inducted

The Coast Guard inducted the first of a planned six ACV fleet meant for shallow water, marshy and mudflat operations, including search-and-rescue and disaster response.

6 JUN 2026 · NEW DELHI

TEJASTRA laser system showcased

The system was displayed as part of the wider push toward indigenous counter-drone and air-defence technology.

12 JUN 2026 · HYDERABAD

Advanced Weapon System Complex inaugurated

At DRDL, the Raksha Mantri credited Akash and BrahMos performance in Operation Sindoor as evidence of India's defence R&D strength.

7 JUN 2026

Zorawar light tank timeline slips

The DRDO-L&T mountain-warfare tank requirements were revised, pushing induction from the earlier target to 2028-29.

29 JUN 2026

Defence Investiture Ceremony

Gallantry awards were conferred for courageous conduct in recent operations and service across the security apparatus.

06

Geopolitical Tensions & India's Perspective

Pakistan

Following the Pahalgam terror attack and Operation Sindoor, India sharpened its response doctrine against cross-border terrorism while keeping its military and diplomatic pressure calibrated.

China

After the 2020 Galwan clash, border management remained a major challenge. India continued to manage the LAC through dialogue and force posture, especially in the face of high-altitude terrain and infrastructure build-up.

Indo-Pacific & strategic autonomy

India pursued strategic autonomy by expanding cooperation with the Quad, France, Australia, and other partners while maintaining the Russia channel for defence and geopolitical balance.

India's key view

The broad strategic principle is that India seeks deterrence without escalation, deterrence through capability, and partnerships that advance national security rather than lock-in any single bloc.

Turning This Into SSB Marks

Likely GD / lecturette topics

  • Tri-service integration and the role of the CDS
  • India's shift from strategic restraint to calibrated response
  • Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence manufacturing
  • Balancing China engagement with border vigilance
  • India's multi-alignment strategy in the Indo-Pacific

Interview — be ready to state cold

  • Current CDS, Service Chiefs and Defence Secretary
  • What the CDS and DMA do that the Chiefs do not
  • One indigenous defence system inducted this year
  • One India-led or India-hosted exercise and its purpose
  • The difference between the LoC and the LAC

Pre-board checklist

  • Can name all three Service Chiefs, the CDS, and the Defence Secretary without hesitation.
  • Can explain why the Army Chief's background matters for command and high-altitude strategy.
  • Can describe Operation Sindoor factually, without falling into rhetoric or speculation.
  • Can list at least one Quad-linked exercise and one neighbourhood-diplomacy exercise.
  • Has verified the names and dates against fresh reporting before the board date.
